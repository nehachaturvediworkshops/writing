on writing

Writing, as a practice and a process, is a container for what one can possibly think and articulate in language.

Writing can have several byproducts and outcomes. It can be a deep confession or a weapon of revolution. Over the years, it has revealed itself to be a space for grounding — for anchoring myself through the wild meanderings of my mind and heart.

It has been a space where I have found myself compulsively trapped and deliriously liberated. For the longest time, I have been drawn to, and biased towards, processes and pathways more than the outcome. To me, my practice is the only thing I can embody and commit to — the rest is all unknown and unseen, and part of a larger, constantly mutating, unraveling whole.

This workshop is an invitation to nurture your entry and commitment to a creative practice that anchors you through the journey of life.

about neha

My journey with writing has been part of a larger creative practice of over two decades which includes visual art, performance, entrepreneurship and community building. An M.Phil in English Literature from the University of Delhi, I have explored writing in academic, journalistic, creative and introspective forms — and I support others working across them.

Besides briefly teaching critical theory at DU and conducting critical-thinking workshops for Master's students in Design at IIT-Bombay, I interned with Zubaan (a feminist publishing house) and wrote for The Times of India as a freelancer in the Response department, with over 300 bylines. My journey extended into filmmaking while working on postgraduate projects at FTII, and later co-directing the award-winning film Our Odyssey is Red.

Some of my writing and art is published in academic and literary journals and anthologies, including Unpsychology magazine, Of Dry Tongues and Brave Hearts, and Doing Things with Stories — Narrative Change. I have co-founded Agra Heritage Walks, the Hers is Ours NGO, and the ISOVI silver jewellery brand, and was a founding member of UP80, a transdisciplinary collaborative residential community in Agra. As a trained Warm Data host who works with modalities such as Collective Alchemy, I bring a particular ability to hold and work with groups. Most recently, I have been exploring AI, the place of technology in our lives, and how dreams and imagination work as portals for speculating, conceptualising and designing desirable futures.
